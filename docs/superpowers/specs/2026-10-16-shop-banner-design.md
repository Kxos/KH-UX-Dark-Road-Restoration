# Shop: draw banners, catalog, unlocks and rotation — design

Status: approved (16-10-2026). Decisions taken with the user in the discussion of 16-10.

## 1. Goal

Make all the original draw banners of the worldwide version (813,
7/4/2016 - 29/6/2021) available in the Shop without modifying the APK, so that:

- the Shop list stays short and readable (catalog with filters instead of 800 plates);
- medals arrive at the pace of the time: a banner unlocks with the story, at the quest released
  in the same period as the banner;
- something happens every week (rotation of timed banners, seasonal ones in their period);
- the tickets obtained (Platinum, Draw Ticket, Spooky Cookie, ...) can be spent.

Success criteria: with the current story (quests 1-525) you draw from the banners up to 11/01/2017 (78);
the Shop shows about ten plates at most; from the catalog you can find any banner and its
state; no Shop button leads to an in-app purchase or to a crash.

## 2. Constraints

- Original APK not modified: only server responses, master tables, downloadable
  resources and WebView pages are changed.
- Repo: only original code and documentation. Banner images, texts and derived icons stay
  outside the repo (reference\banners\img, resources generated by repo scripts).
- Project goal: story up to the final quest 979 (today the server reaches 525). Everything
  that depends on the story uses the numbers 1-979, without caps at 525.
- Every difference from the original must be explained in the Notices (user rule of 16-10).

## 3. Verified facts (tests of 16-10, D:\Progetto_Restauro_KH_UX\tmp\spike_catalog\notes.md)

Client (Shop, `ShopDraw_3Plate_ver340`, plates `ShopDraw_3Plate_Plate_ver310`):

- The list is vertical, ordered by `sortId`; no native tab or filter. The client uses only one
  plate variant (ver310): the draw buttons cannot be hidden.
- `GET /gacha/list` (action 75) is requested **every time the Shop is opened**. Response: `gachaList[]`
  and `petGachaList[]` (mandatory, even empty; without it: «200 ERROR :75»). Mandatory fields of
  `gachaList[]`: validAdd, drawMedalTypeId, drawType, sortId, payType, price, lotCount,
  isDayLimited, userLimitedCount, userWarrantDisplayOfTime, name, description, bannerUrl,
  warrantMedalIds[]; drawTicketlimitDate is also read.
- Each plate requires a row of the master `drawMedalType` with the same id (read without checks:
  without a row, crash). From the row: `detailUrl` (Details button -> our WebView, verified),
  `comment` (scrolling strip under the banner, visible if validComment and
  1 <= userWarrantDisplayOfTime <= warrantDisplayOfTime; it is a printf format string: no loose
  `%`), `benefitId`, `displayTimeLeft` (time-left box), dates.
- `bannerUrl`: 640x140 image downloaded by the client over http from our server.
- Draw Odds opens the global `odds` link of the login (one for all banners).
- Native padlock (`benefitId` != 0 without an active benefit): shows the panel with the padlock and
  disables the draw, but tapping the padlock opens the in-app purchase of a Shop product
 : **do not use it** (crash, or in-app purchase).
- Draw button with tickets (payType 4-9) and tickets < price: grey, the tap does
  nothing and does not reach the server. Price 0 = immediate draw without confirmation: never 0.
  payType 1/3 (jewels) with insufficient funds offers the purchase of jewels. payType 9 crashes
  the Shop (text 106240303 missing).
- «Buy discounted jewels» is hard-wired to the in-app purchase: not reusable.
- Missing textures added in resources 39 (commits 77a1512, 35a8a3b): ShopBut_Lock,
  ShopBut_Lock_Disable, But07_Disable.

Data (outside the repo):

- `reference\banners\banners_ww.tsv` (+ `.md`): 813 banners (680 certain, 132 probable), dates,
  permanent/time-limited state, recurrence, season, event, featured medals, currency, price,
  draw size, guarantees, image (798 with image, 726 already 640x140). Odds
  never published (only those of the abilities).
- `reference\story\story_release_ww.tsv`: worldwide release date of each story tranche
  (1-979, 74 tranches).

## 4. Decisions

### 4.1 «Banner Catalog» plate
First plate, always at the top. Our own 640x140 banner, `comment` with the caption («Open Details to
browse all banners»), `detailUrl` = catalog page. Harmless draw: drawType 1, payType 6
(Gold Ticket, `drawTicket3`, which the server never awards), price 1, benefitId 0. The texts and icon
of the Gold Ticket (106140420-424, `Star7_Ticket_39` with the «仮» mark) are rewritten in the resources
so that the grey box says «Open Details to browse the Banner Catalog» with a suitable icon. If
one day the Gold Ticket were needed, the plate moves to another unused ticket.

### 4.2 Banners available now
A banner is **available** if it is unlocked (4.4) and at this moment:
- it is a **fixture of its era** (4.5), or
- it is **debuting** (7 days from the unlock), or
- it is a **seasonal** banner of the current period (4.6), or
- it is among the **3 in rotation** of the week (4.6).
The others do not appear in the Shop; they are only visible in the catalog.

### 4.3 Shop list (order)
1. Banner Catalog; 2. active seasonal banners (precedence); 3. debuting and in rotation; 4. the **group
chosen** in the catalog, restricted to the available ones; with no choice: **«Novita'»** (New) = available banners unlocked
recently + those payable with the tickets owned. A banner appears only once.

### 4.4 Unlocks with the story
Condition of each banner: «story quest N cleared», with N = the quest preceding the first
of the WW tranche current at the banner's start date (the last row of story_release_ww.tsv with
date <= start: N = first_quest - 1; the launch of 2016-04-07 gives 0, the tranche 201-250 gives 200)
(user decision of 17-10: at the WW launch quests 1-200 came out together and the launch banners
could be drawn from day one). Beyond the restored story (525 today) the banners
stay locked: in the catalog «Unlocks after Story Quest N — coming with a future update». The
rule does not change when quests 526-979 arrive. The tutorial banner stays only in the tutorial.

### 4.5 Fixtures of their era
A `permanent` banner is always available while the **date of the player's point in the story**
(release date of the last quest cleared) falls within its original era (start-end). After the end
of its era it enters the «archive» group of the rotation.

### 4.6 Rotation and seasonal banners
- Every Monday 08:00 UTC (same boundaries as the fake clock) the server chooses **3 banners** among the
  unlocked timed banners, not debuting, not seasonal: **2 from the «recent» group** (start within
  **6 original months** before the date of the point in the story) and **1 from the «archive»** (all the other
  unlocked ones, including the fixtures at the end of their era). In each group the banner that has been idle the longest
  has precedence; the choice is fixed for the whole week and saved (survives restarts). If a group has no
  banners, the slot stays empty (start of the game). 6-month window and number of slots adjustable.
- **Seasonal**: in the period of the season (Halloween, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day, Easter,
  anniversary, summer, ... original dates carried over to the real calendar) the **most recent
  unlocked version** of that seasonal banner appears; none if none is unlocked.
- Seasonal tickets (Spooky Cookie, Holiday Draw Ticket, ...) are spent in the banners of their
  period.

### 4.7 Limited quests and tickets
The Limited campaigns that give tickets (Platinum, Draw Ticket, Holiday Draw Ticket, Moogle Point,
Spooky Cookie) enter the Limited rotation **only when a banner that spends
that ticket is unlocked**. Before that they do not come out (change of `limitedActive`/rotation in server.js).

### 4.8 Draw, odds, guarantees, prices
- Prices, currency, size (x1/x3/x5/x10/x11), daily limit (`isDayLimited`) and featured
  medals: **original** (banners_ww.tsv).
- Odds and guarantees: original where documented, otherwise **reconstructed by banner type**
  (tables by type: normal deal, deal with featured medals, ticket deal, Supernova, 7★, ...). The details
  page and Draw Odds say which are original and which reconstructed.
- Composition of the medals beyond the featured ones («set 1.1» etc.): reconstructed by era from the medals
  released up to that date (plan 2).
- Jewel income: after plan 1 we measure how many jewels a normal player earns in a week
  and adjust the quest and mission rewards (not the prices).

### 4.9 Catalog (WebView)
Page served by our server, opened only from Details of the Catalog plate. Content:
- list of all banners with image, name, original dates, state (available now / in another
  week or season / locked with the condition);
- filters: release period, **anniversary** (Y1 7/4/2016-6/4/2017 ... Y5, Final from 7/4/2021),
  series/theme, contained medal (search), currency, Novita' (New);
- group choice: the server saves it; at the next opening of the Shop that group applies; «Reset»
  goes back to Novita'. If the client does not reload the Shop when the WebView closes, the page says to
  exit and re-enter;
- details of each banner: medals, odds (original/reconstructed), original dates.

### 4.10 Notices
«Shop» notice (INFORMATION) and lines in «Differences from the original game»: catalog from the Details
of the Banner Catalog plate; banners unlocked with the story; weekly rotation and seasonal banners with
precedence; tickets and quests that arrive with their banner; odds reconstructed where
the original did not publish them.

## 5. Architecture

- `server/gacha-data.js` (new): loads the generated banner catalog (row per banner: id, name,
  dates, type, currency, price, size, featured medals, pool, unlock quest, season, image) and the
  odds tables by type.
- `server/make-banners.js` (new): from `reference\banners\banners_ww.tsv` and `story_release_ww.tsv`
  generates the catalog (data file outside the repo if it contains derived data, or our own reduced TSV) and
  the `drawMedalType` rows of the master.
- `server/gacha.js` (new): availability (4.2), weekly rotation saved in `player.gacha`
  (4.6), Shop list (4.3), draw (`POST /gacha/draw`: tickets/jewels, medals, guarantees,
  response with userData/userMedals/firstGetUserMedalIds...), pages `/web/gacha/catalog` and
  `/web/gacha/detail/<id>`, group choice.
- `server/server.js`: routes `/gacha/list`, `/gacha/draw`, `/web/gacha/*`, images `/web/gacha/img/<id>.png`
  (from reference\banners\img, resized to 640x140), `odds` link, Limited filter (4.7).
- Resources: Gold Ticket texts and icon for the Catalog plate (script in the repo, output outside).

## 6. Sub-projects and order

1. **Working draws**: `/gacha/list` + `/gacha/draw`, Banner Catalog plate, tickets and
   jewels, some first-year banners with odds by type; detail page. Tickets
   become spendable.
2. **List of the original banners**: generator from the TSV, master rows, images, pools by era,
   odds tables by type, timed banners with dates.
3. **Catalog, filters, unlocks, rotation, seasonal banners** and Limited filter; Notices.

Each sub-project has its own plan and its own tests (offline + bench).

## 7. Tests

- Offline: availability and rotation at fixed dates (debut, 2+1 slots, precedence to the one idle
  the longest, seasonal banners with the most recent unlocked version, stable choice after a restart);
  unlocks (quest 525 -> only the 78 banners up to 11/01/2017; quest 1 -> only the first ones); draw
  (tickets/jewels deducted once, guarantees, no draw with insufficient funds); Limited
  with tickets excluded until the banner is unlocked.
- Bench: Shop with Catalog + real banners, Details -> catalog, choice of a group -> Shop updated,
  x1/x10 draw with jewels and with tickets, animation and new medals in the list, consistent
  save; no crash in 10 Shop openings.

## 8. Resolved points (16-10)

- **Shop reload when the WebView closes:** bench test brought forward to plan 1. If the
  Shop does not reload, the fallback of 4.9 applies (the page says to exit and re-enter).
- **Pools by era and odds by type:** tables proposed in plan 2 and approved by the user
  before the code.
- **Banners without an end date (60):** rule replaced on 16-10 by decision 9.8 (the notices
  contradict the «same series / 7 days» rule: those deals lasted one day).
- **Small Deal:** two rows. Timed deal 24/6/2016-8/7/2016 (notice 19084) and permanent deal from
  4/7/2016 (the wiki's «07/04/16» in US format) until 14/3/2019.
- **«Common Medal Carnival»:** excluded (no data, WW release not verified).
- **Spirit Blind Box (`/gacha/petdraw`):** outside this specification, its own design after plan 3;
  until then its rows do not appear in the catalog.

## 9. Decisions of sub-project 2 (approved by the user on 16-10)

Full research: `D:\Progetto_Restauro_KH_UX\tmp\sp2\research.md` (supporting files in the same folder).
esearch.md` (supporting files in the same folder).

### 9.1 Types
13 types: SN (Supernova/SN+/SN++) 213, FEATURED (featured medals or guarantee) 168, FALLING (falling price) 129,
SKILL 69, DAILY 64, SEVEN (7★/Prime) 48, FREE 36, STANDARD 23, T_TICKET (Draw Ticket and seasonal
currencies) 21, ITEM (item medals) 20, T_POINTS (Draw Points) 7, BEGINNER 5, T_EVOLVE (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum
tickets) 5. Excluded: 4 Spirit Blind Box and the tutorial banner (B0813). Flags
independent of the type: DAY_LIMITED, VIP, FIRST_FREE, SEASONAL, EVENT, PERMANENT, INCOMPLETE. The 42
rows without price or size are completed from the banner of the same series (11) or from the notice (31);
those that cannot be completed stay only in the catalog.

### 9.2 Reconstructed odds
- Slot composition (rarity_mix), «If bought N» pity and ability odds are
  original; the rest is reconstructed and Draw Odds/details say so («reconstructed»).
- r★ slot: gives r★, with a chance of going up one star only if that star could be drawn at the
  banner's start date: 3->4 10%, 4->5 6%, 5->6 4%, 6->7 3%. Banners with «higher chance ... Epic»
  (Epic Medal Carnival 2016, DAILY/SMALL DEAL Epic): 3★/4★->5★ 15%.
- List slot («one of the following»): uniform, featured medal weight ×2.
- Pity per player and per banner: at the N-th draw without a featured medal the guaranteed slot gives the
  featured medal; «within N draws» the same; «every 3rd draw» = every 3rd draw the list slot.
- Not reproduced: «boosted» medals, Trait Medal, Imitation Medal (they do not exist in the master). Magic
  Mirror and bonus jewels go to the Present Box.
- SKILL: the slot with an ability uses the table of notice 50106; the other medals without an ability.
- T_EVOLVE: Bronze LV1-3, Silver LV4-8 (LV3-8 before 14/09/2018), Gold LV5-8, Platinum Dual Meow
  Wow LV7-9; highest level of the band 10%, the others in equal parts.
- T_TICKET: attack pool of the era + 5★ support medals.

### 9.3 Pools
- Notes «Draws from sets ...» (513 banners): exact union of the sub-sets cited, with the filters cited.
- «Draws from the following medals» (6): explicit list.
- Missing notes (288): list of the last documented banner of the same type (or of any type)
  + standard medals released after it and by the start + guarantee lists.
- Featured medals and guarantee lists are always included.
- Rarities: only those of the rarity_mix, each variant only if released by the start (6★ drawable from
  15/07/2016, 7★ from 25/05/2018, SN from 05/10/2018, SN+ from 2019-09, SN++ from 08/11/2019).
- Permanent banners: pool at the date of the player's point in the story, limited to [start, end].
- Medals with id >= 300000 are included (without them, 448 banners would have empty rarities); bench
  test of the result scene with these ids.
- Fixes the anachronisms of sub-project 1: B0011 without 6★ and without set 2.2; B0001/B0027 with a pool
  that grows with the story; B0456 with Dual Meow Wow LV7-9.

### 9.4 Free draws
Never price 0: payType 1, price 1 (1 Jewel really deducted). isDayLimited where the original had
«once per day». FIRST_FREE and FALLING: dynamic price per player in `/gacha/list` (counter of the
banner's draws in `player.gacha`): 1st draw 1 Jewel, then full price or the price step.

### 9.5 VIP
- 39 VIP banners (rows) with a normal twin on the same date: hidden (in the catalog «VIP variant, not
  reproduced»). The pairs are 41: B0552 and B0554 have two twins (catalog count, 17-10).
- 29 without a twin: normal banners for everyone.
- 17 Free Deal VIP 2020-21: kept, but **only one free draw per day across all the
  active ones**, and **balanced like all the other banners** (user request): same unlock with the
  story (4.4), pool and rarities of their date (9.3), odds from the tables of 9.2, no extra help
  compared with the original. This way they do not give strong or high-level medals at the start of the game.

### 9.6 Gold Ticket
Option A: Ticket Draw Bronze/Silver/Gold only in the catalog («Requires PvP tickets — coming with a
future update») until PvP exists; the Catalog plate stays on payType 6. With PvP: option B
(check whether the button texts depend on the drawType and separate Catalog and Gold Ticket Draw);
fallback C (no Gold Ticket, Gold Ticket Draw paid with 2 Silver Tickets, explained in the Notices).

### 9.7 Seasonal currencies and Draw Points
Spooky Cookie, Moogle Point, Holiday Draw Ticket and Draw Ticket use payType 8 (limitedDrawTicket2):
separate counters in the save; the list never lists together payType 8 banners of different
currencies, and `limitedDrawTicket2` reports the counter of the listed currency. Prices in the original
units. Draw Points (payType 9, crashes today because of the missing text 106240303): postponed, catalog
only.

### 9.8 End dates
The 60 rows without an end: 51 take the period from the cached notice; the others of the same series
(weekly Supernova Deals 2019, Free Deal, FFBE Campaign Rewards Draw) last 1 day; Beginner's
B0623 until 11/10/2020, B0735 until 24/02/2021; Draw Points B0794 until 29/06/2021. The catalog
marks the reconstructed dates.

### 9.9 Images
All 640x140 in 256-colour PNG (~32 MB instead of ~129): LANCZOS resizing for the 80 of
other sizes. The 14 missing ones: image of the banner of the same series, or a generated plate (name
on a neutral background, script in the repo, output outside). First a bench test that the client reads a palette
PNG; if not, full-colour PNG.

### 9.10 Pool files
For each banner a recipe (sub-sets, name lists, rarities, reference date) plus the table
of the sub-sets; the server expands it at startup (~0.3 MB instead of 2.7). All derived data stay
outside the repo (`server/game_data/`).
